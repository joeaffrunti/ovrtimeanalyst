About

Ovrtime Analyst covers the NFL, NBA, NHL, UFC, MLB and more. You get game-day cards, highlights and picks from a model we built ourselves.

Every league gets the same three models: moneylines, over/unders and player props. Each one is built only from games that came before, then checked against real betting prices from past seasons.

The models read who's playing and who's out, who picks up their work, recent form, the matchup on the other side, rest and travel, pace, and the weather where it matters. A pick only goes up when our number beats the price by enough, in a market where that has held up in every test period.

Every pick is logged as a paper bet and graded after the game, and the record counts every loss.

Follow @ovrtimeanalyst on Instagram and comment on any post for that day's picks.
