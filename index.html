About

Ovrtime Analyst covers the NFL, NBA, NHL and more. You get game-day cards, highlights and picks from a model we built ourselves.

It reads play-by-play data. Who's getting the targets and carries, who's out tonight and who picks up their work, how fast a team plays, the wind and rain, and the defense on the other side. We tested its rules on every NFL game back to 2018 before trusting them.

NFL picks go up before every Thursday, Sunday and Monday slate. Each one is logged as a paper bet and graded after the final whistle. We started tracking on November 15, 2026, and the record above counts every loss.

Follow @ovrtimeanalyst on Instagram and comment on any post for that day's picks.
