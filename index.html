About

Ovrtime Analyst covers the NFL, NBA, NHL, UFC, MLB and more. You get game-day cards, highlights and picks from a model we built ourselves.

Every sport gets the same treatment: ratings built only from games that came before, then checked against real betting prices from past seasons. A rule only makes picks once it holds up in every test period.

  • NFL: play-by-play player and team model, tested on every game back to 2018.
  • NBA: player props (points, rebounds, assists, threes) from projected minutes and per-minute rates, checked against real prop prices since 2023.
  • NHL: moneylines from expected-goals ratings, only once both starting goalies are confirmed.
  • UFC: fighter ratings from every UFC fight since 2008: strikes landed and absorbed, takedowns, knockdowns, finish rates, age, reach and layoffs. No UFC rule has passed our tests yet, so UFC is watch-only until one does.

It reads play-by-play data. Who's getting the targets and carries, who's out tonight and who picks up their work, how fast a team plays, the wind and rain, and the defense on the other side. We tested its rules on every NFL game back to 2018 before trusting them.

NFL picks go up before every Thursday, Sunday and Monday slate. Each one is logged as a paper bet and graded after the final whistle, and the record above counts every loss.

Follow @ovrtimeanalyst on Instagram and comment on any post for that day's picks.
